Anthropic Leadership Team

As of — • Version 0

Overview

Anthropic is an American artificial intelligence safety company founded in 2021 by siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei, along with six other former OpenAI researchers. The company is structured as a public benefit corporation (PBC) and is headquartered in San Francisco, California. Anthropic develops the Claude family of AI assistants and is one of the leading AI research organizations globally. In February 2026, the company closed a $30 billion Series G funding round at a $380 billion post-money valuation, led by Coatue and GIC [s1][s2][s17].

The leadership team combines deep expertise in AI research, safety, and scaling with experienced operators from major technology companies. Anthropic's IPO prospectus names seven co-founders, all still with the company; an eighth early leader, Ben Mann, is often called a co-founder but is not part of the founders' voting structure. Since late 2024, Anthropic has expanded its executive ranks with hires across finance, product, commercial, infrastructure, security, public sector, and global affairs. As of September 30, 2026, the company had approximately 5,300 employees [s1][s3][s29].

On October 26, 2026, Anthropic filed a registration statement with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission for an initial public offering on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker ANT. An amended filing on November 10 set a price range of $70 to $78 per share for 825 million shares, which would value the company at up to about $1.4 trillion. CNBC reported that pricing was expected on November 18 and trading on November 19. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley lead the underwriting group. The filing formally lists only three executive officers: CEO Dario Amodei, President Daniela Amodei, and CFO Krishna Rao [s29][s30].

2026 also brought a public dispute with the U.S. government. In late February, the Department of Defense moved to designate Anthropic a "supply chain risk" after the company refused to drop its restrictions on using Claude for mass domestic surveillance and fully autonomous weapons. Anthropic sued in March, and on March 26 U.S. District Judge Rita Lin blocked the designation, calling the government's conduct "classic illegal First Amendment retaliation." Pentagon officials said in September that they still regarded the company as a supply chain risk [s31][s32][s33].

Co-Founders

Dario Amodei — CEO & Co-Founder

Dario Amodei is an AI researcher and entrepreneur serving as CEO, co-founder, and director of Anthropic. He holds a B.S. in Physics from Stanford University and a Ph.D. in Biophysics from Princeton University, and completed a postdoctoral fellowship at Stanford University School of Medicine. Before founding Anthropic, Amodei worked at Baidu (2014–2015) and as a Senior Research Scientist at Google Brain (2015–2016), then joined OpenAI in July 2016, where he rose to Vice President of Research [s4][s29].

Dario departed OpenAI in December 2020 over concerns about the pace of commercialization relative to safety research, and co-founded Anthropic in January 2021. He has been a prominent voice advocating for responsible AI development while pursuing frontier model capabilities [s4][s5][s29].

In 2026 Amodei led Anthropic through its dispute with the Department of Defense and its IPO preparations. On September 12, 2026, he published an essay, "We Must Pace the Frontier," calling on AI companies to slow the rate at which model capabilities improve so that safety work can keep up. He proposed that independent evaluators get ongoing, employee-like access to frontier labs, and said Anthropic would offer this itself [s31][s34].

Daniela Amodei — President & Co-Founder

Daniela Amodei serves as President and co-founder of Anthropic. She graduated from University of California, Santa Cruz with a Bachelor of Arts in English Literature, having originally attended on a scholarship for classical flute performance. Her early career spanned global health and politics, including managing communications for U.S. Representative Matt Cartwright [s6].

In 2013, Daniela joined Stripe as an early employee, working as a Risk Manager among other roles until 2018. She then moved to OpenAI, where she held positions including Engineering Manager, Vice President of People, and Vice President of Safety and Policy. She co-founded Anthropic in January 2021 with her brother Dario and oversees the company's business operations, go-to-market strategy, policy, and people functions. Several senior leaders report to her, including the CTO, the Labs co-leads, and the Chief Global Affairs Officer. The IPO prospectus also names her Chairperson of Anthropic's board of directors [s6][s19][s26][s29][s35].

Other Co-Founders

Anthropic's IPO prospectus names five further co-founders, all former OpenAI staff. Together with the Amodei siblings, these seven will be the sole members of a "Founder LLC" that holds a single share of Class F stock carrying 50.1% of shareholder voting power, including the vote on directors not chosen by the Long-Term Benefit Trust. Each co-founder has also pledged to give 80% of their personal Anthropic equity to philanthropic causes [s29]:

Tom Brown — Co-Founder & Chief Compute Officer. Brown led the engineering of GPT-3 at OpenAI and co-invented deep RLHF with Dario Amodei. At Anthropic he leads the technical organization that secures, scales, and uses the company's compute resources [s29].

Jack Clark — Co-Founder, Head of the Anthropic Institute. Clark leads the Anthropic Institute, an effort to share research from across the company with external partners and the public. He previously served as Policy Director at OpenAI, worked as a technology journalist for The Register and BusinessWeek, and writes the "Import AI" newsletter. He is expected to be one of the initial managers of the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund [s29].

Jared Kaplan — Co-Founder & Chief Science Officer. Kaplan leads Anthropic's scientific research agenda. A theoretical physicist, he was an associate professor at Johns Hopkins University, is a principal author of the neural scaling laws, and helped pioneer Constitutional AI [s29].

Sam McCandlish — Co-Founder & Chief Architect. McCandlish served as Anthropic's original CTO and transitioned to the Chief Architect role in October 2025, where he focuses on pre-training and large-scale model architecture. He previously built and led a team at OpenAI studying the science and scaling of machine learning [s19][s29].

Christopher Olah — Co-Founder & Head of Interpretability. Olah is a leading researcher in mechanistic interpretability—the science of understanding neural networks by reverse-engineering their internal representations into human-understandable algorithms. He previously led interpretability research at OpenAI and was a research scientist at Google Brain [s8][s9][s29].

Ben Mann — Founding team member. Mann is a software engineer who helped build GPT-3 at OpenAI. The prospectus says he has been "an integral part of Anthropic since its founding" and is "often referred to as a co-founder," but he is not a member of the Founder LLC. He founded the Labs team in mid-2024 to bridge research and end-user products, and co-leads the expanded Labs division [s20][s29].

Executive Team

Krishna Rao — Chief Financial Officer

Krishna Rao has served as Anthropic's first Chief Financial Officer since April 2024, and is one of the three executive officers named in the IPO prospectus. He joined from Fanatics Commerce, where he was CFO from 2023 to 2024, after serving as CFO of the healthcare technology company Cedar (2021–2023). From 2015 to 2021 he held roles at Airbnb including Global Head of Corporate & Business Development. He earlier worked at Blackstone and Bain, and holds a J.D. from Yale Law School and an A.B. in Economics from Harvard College [s10][s11][s29].

Rahul Patil — Chief Technology Officer

Rahul Patil joined Anthropic as Chief Technology Officer in October 2025, succeeding co-founder Sam McCandlish. Patil previously served as CTO at Stripe, where he led engineering and global operations for over five years, and held prior leadership roles at Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, Amazon Web Services, and Microsoft Azure. He oversees Anthropic's engineering organization across product, compute, infrastructure, inference, data science, and security, reporting to President Daniela Amodei [s19][s21].

Paul Smith — Chief Commercial Officer

Paul Smith joined Anthropic as its first Chief Commercial Officer in August 2025. He brings over 30 years of experience building and scaling global go-to-market organizations at Microsoft, Salesforce, and ServiceNow, where he most recently served as President of Global Customer and Field Operations. Smith leads sales, partnerships, revenue operations, and enterprise marketing [s22][s23].

Ami Vora — Chief Product Officer

Ami Vora joined Anthropic in late 2025 as Head of Product and took over the Product organization in January 2026, following Mike Krieger's transition to the Labs division. She is now described as Chief Product Officer. She previously spent about fifteen years at Meta, including as Vice President of Product at Facebook and Vice President of Product and Design at WhatsApp, and was Chief Product Officer at Faire. Vora focuses on scaling Claude for enterprise and consumer markets [s24][s25].

Mariano-Florentino Cuéllar — Chief Global Affairs Officer

Mariano-Florentino "Tino" Cuéllar became Anthropic's first Chief Global Affairs Officer on August 4, 2026. He leads the company's policy, international engagement, and government relations work, and reports to President Daniela Amodei. Cuéllar is a former Justice of the Supreme Court of California and recently stepped down as president of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace. He had served as a trustee of Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust since January 2026 and left that seat to take the job [s35][s36].

Mike Krieger — Co-Lead, Labs

Mike Krieger, co-founder of Instagram, joined Anthropic as Chief Product Officer in 2024. In January 2026, Krieger transitioned to co-lead Anthropic's Labs division alongside co-founder Ben Mann, reporting to President Daniela Amodei. Labs is an internal incubator responsible for experimental products at the frontier of Claude's capabilities, including Claude Code and MCP [s12][s26].

Jan Leike — Head of Alignment Science

Jan Leike joined Anthropic in mid-2024 to co-lead the Alignment Science team, departing from OpenAI where he had led the Superalignment team. Leike holds a PhD in reinforcement learning theory from the Australian National University and previously worked at DeepMind, where he prototyped reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) techniques [s2][s13].

Security Leadership

Vitaly Gudanets — Chief Information Security Officer. Gudanets joined Anthropic in late 2025, bringing over 30 years of experience across security engineering, privacy, and governance from leadership roles at Netflix, Google, Zendesk, and Symantec [s27].

Jason Clinton — Deputy CISO. Clinton was Anthropic's first CISO, joining in April 2023 after more than a decade at Google leading Chrome infrastructure security. He transitioned to Deputy CISO following Gudanets's appointment [s27].

Commercial Leadership

Chris Ciauri — Managing Director, International. Ciauri joined Anthropic to lead the company's global expansion outside of the United States, building on an enterprise sales background [s3].

Guillaume Princen — Head of EMEA. Princen oversees Anthropic's European, Middle Eastern, and African operations [s3].

Hidetoshi Tojo — Head of Japan. Tojo leads Anthropic's market efforts in Japan [s3].

Kate Earle Jensen — Head of Americas. Jensen leads Anthropic's commercial operations in the Americas region [s3].

Irina Ghose — Managing Director, India. Ghose was appointed in January 2026 to lead Anthropic's business in India. She previously led Microsoft's India business, ending a 24-year career there in December 2025 [s38].

Teresa Carlson — Global Head of Public Sector. Carlson joined in July 2026 as Anthropic's first global head of public sector, overseeing work with governments and public institutions in the United States, the United Kingdom, and other markets. She founded AWS's Worldwide Public Sector business in 2010, held public sector roles at Microsoft and Splunk, and most recently led the General Catalyst Institute [s37].

Thiyagu Ramasamy — Public Sector. Ramasamy joined in January 2025 from Amazon Web Services, where he led data, analytics, and AI/ML initiatives for public sector customers, to build Anthropic's go-to-market strategy for federal, state, and local government agencies [s28].

Notable Departures

Several researchers left in 2026 with public statements about AI risk. In February, Mrinank Sharma, head of the Safeguards Research team, announced his departure in a public letter; Anthropic noted he did not lead the company's broader safety or safeguards work [s39]. In September, researcher Jacob Coxon resigned with a widely shared post warning that Anthropic and OpenAI were "racing straight to self-improving superintelligence" [s40]. Board member Jay Kreps resigned in May 2026 (see below) [s29].

Board of Directors

According to Anthropic's IPO prospectus, the board had six members as of October 31, 2026. After the offering, directors elected by the Long-Term Benefit Trust through Class T stock (marked "Class T" below) will make up a majority of the board, and holders of Class A and Class F stock will elect the rest [s29]:

Dario Amodei — Co-Founder and CEO of Anthropic. Director since 2021 (Classes A and F) [s29].

Daniela Amodei — Co-Founder and President of Anthropic, and Chairperson of the board. Director since 2021 (Classes A and F) [s29].

Reed Hastings — Lead independent director (Class T). Hastings co-founded Netflix and was its CEO from 1999 to 2023 and chairman until June 2026. He joined Anthropic's board in May 2025 and sits on its audit committee and its nominating and governance committee [s14][s29].

Chris Liddell — Director since February 2026 (Class T). A former CFO of Microsoft, General Motors, and International Paper, and former Deputy White House Chief of Staff (2017–2021), Liddell is a director of Commonwealth Fusion Systems and the Council on Foreign Relations. He sits on the audit and the safety and risk committees [s15][s29].

Vas Narasimhan — Director since April 2026 (Class T). Narasimhan has been CEO of Novartis since 2018 and is the first pharmaceutical executive on Anthropic's board. He was appointed by the Long-Term Benefit Trust, and his arrival gave Trust-appointed directors a majority of seats. He sits on the compensation and the safety and risk committees [s29][s41].

Yasmin Razavi — Director since March 2023 (Classes A and F). Razavi is a General Partner at Spark Capital, one of Anthropic's early investors [s29].

Former director: Jay Kreps, co-founder and CEO of Confluent, joined the board in May 2024 as a Trust appointee and resigned in May 2026 [s16][s29].

Long-Term Benefit Trust

Anthropic maintains a Long-Term Benefit Trust (LTBT), a purpose trust established for "the responsible development and maintenance of advanced AI for the long-term benefit of humanity." The LTBT holds Class T shares, which carry no economic rights but let it elect and remove a majority of the board. The Trust has five authorized seats for financially disinterested trustees, one of which was vacant as of the November 2026 prospectus [s2][s29].

The current trustees are Neil "Buddy" Shah, CEO of the Clinton Health Access Initiative and Chair of the Trust since June 2023; Richard Fontaine, CEO of the Center for a New American Security, since April 2025; former Federal Reserve Chair Ben Bernanke, whose appointment was announced in July 2026; and Harvard physicist Christopher Stubbs, since October 2026. Earlier trustees Kanika Bahl and Zach Robinson were no longer on the Trust by mid-2026, and Tino Cuéllar served from January to August 2026 before joining the company as Chief Global Affairs Officer [s29][s35][s42].

Research Leadership

Anthropic's research organization is led by several co-founders and senior researchers who drive the company's work on frontier AI capabilities and safety [s2][s8]:

Jared Kaplan (Chief Science Officer) sets the overall scientific direction, drawing on his background in physics and neural scaling laws. His research on how model performance scales with compute, data, and parameters has been foundational to the field [s7].

Sam McCandlish (Chief Architect, formerly CTO) focuses on pre-training and large-scale model architecture, having transitioned from the CTO role in October 2025 to allow dedicated attention to the challenge of training ever-larger models [s7][s19].

Christopher Olah heads the interpretability research team, pioneering techniques to understand what neural networks learn and how they process information internally. His work on mechanistic interpretability is considered foundational to the field of AI safety [s8][s9].

Jan Leike leads the Alignment Science team, which focuses on ensuring AI systems behave in accordance with human intentions. His prior work on RLHF at DeepMind and superalignment research at OpenAI directly informs Anthropic's approach to AI safety [s13].

In January 2026, Anthropic expanded its "Labs" division, an internal incubator originally founded by Ben Mann in mid-2024. Former CPO Mike Krieger now co-leads Labs alongside Mann, with the team responsible for experimental products including Claude Code, MCP, and Cowork. The division reports to President Daniela Amodei and is actively hiring to double its headcount [s12][s20][s26].

Tom Brown (Chief Compute Officer) leads the organization that secures and scales the compute used to train and serve Anthropic's models [s29].

Senior Research Hires in 2026

Anthropic recruited several prominent researchers in 2026. Andrej Karpathy, an OpenAI founding member and former director of AI at Tesla, announced on May 19 that he had joined Anthropic to return to research, reportedly on the pre-training team [s43][s44]. In June, John Jumper, who shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for AlphaFold, announced he was leaving Google DeepMind after nearly nine years to join Anthropic. Fellow AlphaFold researchers Jonas Adler and Alexander Pritzel followed. Fortune linked the hires to Dario Amodei's stated plan to do more work in biology [s45][s46].
